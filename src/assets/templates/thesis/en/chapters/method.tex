\section{Method}

The method relies on the model
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:model}
  \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = -\lambda x, \qquad x(0) = x_0,
\end{equation}
whose solution is $x(t) = x_0 e^{-\lambda t}$.
