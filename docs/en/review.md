# Review

*[Français](../fr/relecture.md)*

As on Overleaf, you can comment on a passage, and track changes so that
they are accepted or rejected.

## Commenting

Select a passage: an **Add Comment** button appears next to it (shortcut
**Alt+C**). Write the comment, then **Add Comment**. The comments button
(the speech bubble, with their number), in the editor's toolbar, opens the
panel where comments are answered (**Add response**) and marked as
resolved. Readers do not comment; editors and reviewers do.

### Being told by email

A reply in a comment thread tells those who wrote in it by email; writing
`@` and an account name, in a comment or a reply, tells that collaborator
too. So do replies to tracked changes, those written in the **Review**
overview (on the left) and those of [Claude](claude.md).

The email quotes the passage, the comment and the reply, and gives the
document's link. Only the project's members whose account email is
confirmed receive it; untick **Review emails** in your profile to get no
more. So as to flood nobody, an account gets at most five a day.

## Tracking changes

The track changes button (the pen) turns tracking on for you. Its arrow
opens the **Tracking Options**: track the changes of **Everyone**, or of
chosen people. This setting holds for the whole project and for all its
collaborators, as on Overleaf.

Added text shows underlined in green, removed text struck through in red.

## Accepting or rejecting

- **In the text**: click a change. A small **Accept / Reject** bar appears
  just above it, and the **Changes** panel opens at its card. The bar goes
  away when you type.
- **In the Changes panel** (the button with the number of changes, in the
  editor's toolbar): each card has its **Accept** and **Reject** buttons; at
  the top, **Accept all**, **Reject all** and **Resolve all** apply to the
  open file.
- **For the whole project**: the **Review overview** tab of the left column
  lists the comments and changes of every file.

## Reviewers

Someone invited with **Can review** comments, answers and suggests changes,
always tracked; they neither accept nor reject them. The server refuses any
change of theirs that is not tracked.

The changes [Claude](claude.md) suggests arrive as tracked changes too,
under its name.
